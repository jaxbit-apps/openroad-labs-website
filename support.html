Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my data?

Email us at flockhopper@proton.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we'll process your request within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does FlockHopper track my location?

No. All routing calculations happen on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your GPS location or route history. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID settings. Go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your iPhone, then select FlockHopper to cancel or modify your plan.

How accurate is the ALPR data?

Camera locations are crowdsourced by volunteers on OpenStreetMap. Cameras get installed and moved often, so some entries will be out of date. If you spot an error, please tell us, or fix it directly on OpenStreetMap.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email flockhopper@proton.me with a description of the issue, your device type, and screenshots if you have them.
